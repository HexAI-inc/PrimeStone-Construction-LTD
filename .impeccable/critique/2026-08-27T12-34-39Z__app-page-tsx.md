## Overall Impression

Typography and section rhythm are decent; the scroll-reveal system is better-factored than most scaffold code. Underneath, the page sells a company that does not exist to an audience uniquely equipped to check. Biggest opportunity: this page would convert better with less on it.

## What's Working

1. **Reading comfort survives a bad connection.** Consistent container/py-20/alternating bands, Poppins with leading-relaxed and max-w-3xl subheads. When Unsplash fails, the page still reads as a coherent document.
2. **The scroll-reveal system is disciplined.** AnimatedSection.tsx:24 and StaggeredContainer.tsx:16 use useInView(once: true); inline motion.divs follow. Needs a reduced-motion guard, not a rewrite.
3. **Real contact details in persistent chrome.** Navigation.tsx:40-48 puts the genuine phone and email on every viewport. Correct instinct for a business whose only verified assets are contact details.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Every CTA leads to a form that silently discards the enquiry while promising a reply
page.tsx:191 → /contact, page.tsx:468 → /quote. Both console.log and alert() a promise; quote/page.tsx:86 promises 24 hours, echoed in that page's hero. Both buttons share the label "Get Free Quote" but differ in destination; nav offers a third label.
**Why:** A client completes a four-step form at midnight in New York and never hears back. Worse than no form. PRODUCT.md forbids promising a reply until WhatsApp handoff exists.
**Fix:** One label, one destination. Strip response-time promises. Make submit open WhatsApp with the payload prefilled.
**Command:** /impeccable harden app/quote/page.tsx

### [P1] The credibility layer is fabricated, checkable in under a minute by this audience
page.tsx:308-311 (500+/15+/100%/50+), six sets of per-service counts and ratings, ISO 9001 / 50+ Experts / 98% On-Time / Zero Incidents badges, three testimonials with Unsplash portraits, four projects with Unsplash photography.
**Why:** Per-service counts sum to 610 vs "500+". "15+ Years" vs nav's "Since 2020". "100% Satisfaction" vs 4.9★ and 4.8★ on the same page. About says 50+ and ISO 9001:2015 vs this page's 500+ and a 9001:2022 elsewhere. "15 Floors — Banjul" is checkable by anyone who knows Banjul; every image reverse-searches to Unsplash. Each catch discounts the real phone number too.
**Fix:** Delete, do not soften. Remove per-service stats, benefit stats, the Track Record panel, and the testimonials section. Replace the projects grid with references-on-request or actual services.
**Command:** /impeccable clarify app/page.tsx

### [P1] WhatsApp absent from the homepage; terminal CTA is an international phone call
WhatsApp appears zero times in app/page.tsx. contact/page.tsx:221 points at wa.me/2201234567, neither real number. Hours only in the footer, Saturday dropped, no timezone stated anywhere.
**Why:** PRODUCT.md names WhatsApp as the handoff channel and notes this visitor browses outside Gambian hours.
**Fix:** WhatsApp primary in hero and closing CTA with the real number, prefilled message, and an honest availability line stating GMT. tel: secondary. Fix the placeholder wa.me link now.
**Command:** /impeccable clarify app/page.tsx

### [P1] Every specific about the work is locked behind mouse-hover
FlipCard.tsx:19-20 triggers on mouse enter/leave only — no onClick, onFocus, tabIndex, role. Ten cards use it. Both faces in the DOM with no aria-hidden. page.tsx:353 puts cursor-pointer on non-clickable cards.
**Why:** Heavily mobile audience; on touch every stat and project detail is unreachable behind cards that look interactive.
**Fix:** Drop FlipCard from the homepage and show one honest face; elsewhere make it a real button with onClick, onKeyDown, aria-expanded, aria-hidden on the inactive face.
**Command:** /impeccable adapt app/page.tsx

### [P1] Contrast failures on the primary CTA, plus no reduced-motion escape
.btn-primary is white on #D2691E→#EA580C = 3.56–3.63:1, failing AA normal along its length, reaching 4.76 only on hover — the resting state is the worse one. Below the 3:1 large-text floor: text-yellow-400 stars on white 1.53:1; gradient text 2.24:1 against the hero mid stop; text-orange-300 on orange-800 2.68:1. ~50 animated elements render; repo-wide grep for prefers-reduced-motion/useReducedMotion/motion-safe/motion-reduce returns nothing. **Fix:** Darken the button gradient to its hover values at rest. Replace text-orange-300 on orange with a light neutral. Add a prefers-reduced-motion block; gate scroll-behavior: smooth behind it.
