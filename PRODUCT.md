## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: Gambian diaspora abroad.** People living in the UK, US, or EU who are building or renovating property back home in The Gambia and cannot be on site. They are in a different timezone from the build, cannot inspect work themselves, are often sending money to a country they visit once a year or less, and are choosing a contractor almost entirely on trust signals they can evaluate from a screen. Their job when they arrive on this site: decide whether this company can be trusted with a large sum of money and years of absence.

Other audiences the company serves commercially — local homeowners, business owners, property developers, institutional/civil clients — are real customers but were **not** confirmed as the audience this site is designed to win. Do not treat them as co-primary without the user saying so.

## Product Purpose

Primestone Construction Company Ltd. is a construction company operating in The Gambia, offering residential construction, commercial construction, renovation, civil engineering, and site development.

The website's job is to convert a remote enquiry into a contact — its success measure is a qualified project enquiry reaching the company, not page views.

## Positioning

**Remote-client delivery** — being the contractor a client can use without ever standing on the site.

This is the company's chosen position and the claim all future work should serve. It is currently **aspirational, not operational**: as of 2026-08-27 there is no formal process for keeping an absent client informed — no defined update cadence, no reporting format, no milestone-payment structure. Future work may position around remote clients and may state the intent, but must not describe a progress-reporting workflow, update schedule, client portal, or payment-milestone system as something that exists. If such a workflow is built, record it here and the claim becomes evidence-backed.

## Operating Context

- The client and the construction site are in different countries and time zones. Decisions, approvals, and payments happen at a distance.
- Enquiry handoff is **WhatsApp**: the quote and contact forms should route the enquiry to WhatsApp rather than to a backend. This suits both local and diaspora clients and requires no server.
- Stated business hours: Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM, Sat 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM (The Gambia, GMT). A diaspora visitor is frequently browsing outside these hours.
- Inferred, not confirmed: significant mobile use and variable bandwidth on the Gambian side. Treat as a design consideration, not a stated requirement.

## Product Principles

1. **Trust is the product.** The primary visitor is committing a large sum from thousands of miles away. Every design decision is judged by whether it makes that commitment feel safer or riskier.
2. **Never invent proof.** No fabricated counts, ratings, certifications, testimonials, client names, or project photography. An honest absence beats a manufactured credential — and for a real business, a fake credential is a liability.
3. **Design for the gap, not around it.** Real evidence is thin today. Surfaces should be built so that genuine proof drops in as it arrives, rather than being padded with placeholder credibility.
4. **The distance is the brief.** Time zones, WhatsApp, absent clients, and a country the visitor may not have seen in years are the real conditions of use — not an edge case.
5. **Every claim needs an owner.** Anything stated as fact must trace back to something recorded in this file. If it isn't here, it isn't true yet.
